Express Eviction

Input standard input
Output standard output
Time limit 1 second
Memory limit 256 megabytes

Bajtocja is a rectangular land consisting of H⋅WH \cdot W cells arranged in HH rows and WW columns. The border of each cell is a local road with little traffic (this includes the outer border of the land). Each cell is either empty or inhabited by one resident. The residents like peace and would not want to live right next to a planned express road.

Your task is to plan the route of the express road from the upper-left corner to the lower-right corner of Bajtocja. The route can only run along existing roads, i.e. along cell borders. The length and the number of turns of the route do not matter.

Every resident whose cell is adjacent to the express road — by a side or even only by a corner — has to be evicted. In other words, a resident is evicted if the road passes through at least one of the four corner points of their cell.

What is the minimum number of residents that need to be evicted?

Input

The first line contains two integers HH and WW — the dimensions of Bajtocja.

Each of the next HH lines contains a string of length WW consisting of characters . (empty cell) and # (inhabited cell).

Output

Output a single integer — the minimum possible number of evicted residents.

Constraints

  • 1≤H,W≤501 \le H, W \le 50
  • Each row contains exactly WW characters, each of them . or #.
